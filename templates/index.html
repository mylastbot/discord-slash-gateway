How the Focus Game Works

The focus game helps users stay focused and productive:

  1. Start a focus session with /focus
  2. Points accumulate while you're in focus mode
  3. The longer you focus, the higher your multiplier goes
  4. End your session with /unfocus to see your score
  5. Compete with others on the leaderboard!
